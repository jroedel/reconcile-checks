Reading it

  1. Hold the calibration page against a blank check, edges together, up to a window or a lamp.
  2. Look at the boxes for the date, the payee, the amount and the memo. Each should sit on the check's own line, inside its space.
  3. If they all sit a little to one side, measure how far with the rulers along the edges. If the page's boxes are to the right of the check's, the offset to the right gets smaller by that much; if they are below, the offset down gets smaller.
  4. Save the new offset, print the page again, and look again, until it lines up.
  5. Then put the checks back in {{.Account.Tray}}, {{if .Account.Feed}}{{.Account.Feed}}{{else}}the way up you settled{{end}}.
